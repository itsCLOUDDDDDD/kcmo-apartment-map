    About this apartment search

    I’m comparing Kansas City apartments by location, space, cost and everyday comfort. Being able to walk to places I enjoy is one part of the decision—not an automatic winner.

    How to use it

    Choose one or both apartment ZIP codes. Tap a home pin or a photo for details. Open an apartment and choose “Add to comparison.” Then choose one or two more apartments to see them together. A number on a home pin opens a group of properties.

    What the labels mean

    My preference rank is my personal order within each ZIP. Shortest walk is only distance, not a recommendation. Unranked apartments have not been put in a preference order.

    Unverified means we still need to check. Places without confirmed map pins remain in the list. Listed rents and vacancies can change; sources and check dates are in each apartment’s details.

    Build & privacy

    V3.xlsx is the master list. Workbook changes need a rebuild and a separate publication step before the shared link updates. Viewing or comparing does not edit that list. Private housing paperwork is excluded.
